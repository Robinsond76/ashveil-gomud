## Acceptance limitation: shipped human automatic stats

A follow-up run of `TestPhase35StartingClassMeasurements` through the real
human `Character.Validate` path measures automatic adjusted stat changes at
levels **5, 15, 25, 30, 35, 45, 55, 60**: **8 of 59** level-ups from 2–60.
The shipped human racial bases are 0/1; truncation of the approved fractional
formula and stat compression leave most intervening levels unchanged. The
base-10 interpolation example above is formula coverage, not representative
human progression.

The plan's "at least one stat rises on most level-ups" criterion is therefore
**unmet** for shipped humans. The fifth-level parity and monotonicity criteria
pass. This phase retains the approved curve and race data; changing either
requires a design follow-up. HP/mana and training still grow on level-up, with
stat points every two levels. Zone tuning and caster changes remain in 35b.
