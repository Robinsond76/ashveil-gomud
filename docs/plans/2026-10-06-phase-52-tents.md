# Phase 52: tents with trade-offs (execution plan and decisions, 2026-10-06)

Scope is phase 52 of `docs/plans/2026-10-06-outward-survival-phases.md`. Owner
standing rules apply: full autonomy (decide, record why), the world is
temporary, difficulty comes only from zone level, nothing bought resells for
profit.

## Decisions

1. **Four tents, one pitched at a time.** Oiled canvas (item 46, unchanged),
   fur-lined (300), camouflaged (301), large pavilion (302). Items are 300-302,
   well clear of the highest shipped id in `other-0` (290). The model is
   `internal/camping/tents.go` (`Tents`, `TentOf`, `PickTent`, `ScaleChance`).
2. **Effects, each real and tested:**
   - *Canvas:* as before (halves the weather's penalty to a rest, cold off).
   - *Fur:* the weather's penalty to a rest's fatigue recovery is gone, not
     halved (`campRecovery`'s `fullShelter`). Why: "no cold penalty during
     the rest" is the one place a rest takes a cold or wet penalty; every
     tent already keeps the room's cold off (`RoomWarmedByFire`).
   - *Camouflaged:* raider and thief chance x50%. The spec's "no Well Rested"
     is moot (a camp rest grants Rested; only an inn grants Well Rested), so
     the cost is a light sleep: **Rested lasts half as long** (7m30s).
   - *Large:* raider and thief chance x150% (capped 100). The spec's "Well
     Rested for everyone" is a camp rest granting the **Well Rested buff**
     (30m, +2 speed/perception) instead of Rested, for everyone who slept.
     Only the buff upgrades: wounds, mana and vitals still follow a camp rest
     (an inn's free wound knitting would make a bought tent a cheap inn).
     Members on a duty still miss it (51).
3. **Chances scale in the same single roll** at rest start
   (`planRaidLocked`, `planTheftLocked`), so a restart never re-rolls and the
   existing roll order in tests holds. Bells still stop thieves whatever the
   tent.
4. **Choice: `camp tent [kind|clear]`.** The leader chooses among the tents
   the company carries (`Registry.TentChoices`, persisted); no choice pitches the
   first carried in `Tents` order (canvas first, so a player with only the
   old tent sees no change). The choice outlives the camp, like auto-sharpen
   (52 review: a company carrying two tents had to re-choose at every camp),
   and can be made before camping. A
   chosen tent no longer carried falls back. The pitched tent is locked on the
   rest (`RestSession.Tent`) and cannot change mid-rest. Why a command rather
   than "best carried": the tents are trade-offs, not a ladder.
5. **Pending-grant safety (lesson from 51).** The Rested grant runs on the
   next round, so the tent that sets its buff is saved with the pending
   marker (`Registry.RestedTents`, rolled back on a failed save, purged,
   test-area snapshotted), never read from the live camp.
6. **Never sold back.** Market lines are `SupplyOnly` (the no-buyback
   switch) at both provisioners; thieves never take tents (`campGearItem`).
   Item values equal the base prices, so NPC merchants (25% of value) are
   far below cost too. A test walks the market config.
7. **Weight counts against load.** Tents are ordinary items: 7 / 9 / 12 / 20
   kg. The 20 kg pavilion needs a horse of its own, and help says so.
8. **UI.** `Company.Camp` GMCP gains `tent_kind`, `tent_name`, `tent_note`
   and `tents` (carried, with the pitched one marked, and the command). The
   Camp tab names the pitched tent and its effect, and shows a Tent picker
   below the camp buttons (as 51 learned) when more than one is carried;
   disabled while resting. `camp status`, the rest-start gear line, `look`
   and the camp-gear view name the tent.
9. **Help and tutorial.** `help camp gear` gains a Tents section; `help
   camp`, `campwatch`, `inn` updated; aliases `tents`, `camp tent`, `fur
   tent`...; the camp-gear tutorial hint names the tents and `camp tent`.
