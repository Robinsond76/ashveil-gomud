# Phase 74: Rites for the dead

When a companion is lost for good, or walks away after long service, the company wants to mourn them. The next camp or inn stay announces it; the leader holds the rites or lets the loss pass. Holding gives a chronicle line, steadies those who trusted the one gone and draws them together; letting it pass costs loyalty. Spec: [Pillars phases](2026-10-07-pillars-phases.md) §74.

## Ordering: no dependency on faiths

The plan makes 74 depend on 65 (bonds), not on 73 (creeds), so it is built whole. It names no god, faith or creed; the world's lore is unsettled ([style bible](../designs/world-style-bible.md): magic is rare and feared). **Seam for 73:** `internal/rites` owns the words (`Phrase`, `Verb`) and the numbers; a creed can add words to `riteIntro` or pick another intro line, and can add a source of loyalty or bond change beside `Steady` and `Grieve`, without changing when a rite is queued or what it costs.

## Shape

- **Rules** in `internal/rites` (pure): `Cause` (lost, left), `Mourns`, `Steady`, `Grieve`, the numbers.
- **State** on the record: `Record.Rites` (`internal/company/rite.go`): the departed's number, name, level, cause, who was close (`Close`) and the rest of the band (`Band`) at the loss, and `Offered`. Queued in memory by `queueRite` just before `dropCompanion` and written by the departure's own save, so the loss and the occasion are one write; a failed departure takes the rite back (`unqueueRite`).
- **Sources:** `expire` (a lost companion, `rites.Lost`) and `removeCompanion` (every caller is a desertion, `rites.Left`, only past `rites.LongServiceRounds`). Dismissal is the leader's choice and queues nothing.
- **Offer:** `company.OfferRites` (a `RitesProvider` on the company module) is called from the real starts of a camp rest (`startRest`) and an inn stay (`innRest`) and appends its text. It first lets pass any rite an earlier stay already offered, then announces the rest.
- **Commands:** `rites` (read-only, allowed while downed), `rite hold|skip [member|#N|all]`. Holding or skipping needs a camp or inn stay (`camping.LeaderRest`) and no battle.
- **Chronicle:** kind `rites` (words `rites`, `rite`, `funerals`), `Ref` `rite:held` or `rite:skipped`.
- **Bonds:** a new source `bonds.Rite` (1 hour cooldown, +2, rises only to "close").
- **Web:** `Company.Rites` GMCP extra and a Rites sub-tab in the Company window (a card per rite with Hold rites and Let pass). The sub-tab bar now scrolls sideways on a phone (eight tabs outgrew 360 px) and brings the chosen tab into view.
- **Help:** `help rites` (hubs: `adventure`, `company`, `chronicle`, `webclient`, `bonds`, `resurrect`; keywords and aliases), a tutorial hint in the Departure lesson.

## Decisions (best judgment, full autonomy)

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| "Dies for good" is a companion lost when the rescue allowance runs out (`expire`). A companion who falls and is raised is not mourned. | The game's only permanent loss. A raised companion costs a level already. |
| "Leaves after long service" is a desertion (loyalty ran out, a bond rivalry ended) with at least 2,700 rounds of service, the Trusted chemistry tier. A test pins the number to the tier. | Reuses the game's own measure of a long time together; a recruit who leaves after a day is not worth a funeral. |
| No rites for a companion the leader dismisses. | Spec: a death or a leaving; dismissal is the leader's own act, and a rite there would be a loyalty lever for free. |
| Rites are announced at the start of a camp rest or an inn stay, and answered with a command at any pitched camp (during or after its rest) or in any inn room, stay or not (review fix: an inn stay lasts a minute). | "The next camp offers rites"; the real starts are one hook each, where banter and opinions already attach. |
| An offered rite that is still unanswered when the next camp or inn stay starts passes, with the skip cost. Nothing passes the first time it is offered. | Skipping must cost, but a leader who never saw the offer (logged out mid-rest) gets one whole camp to answer. No timers, nothing hidden: the list says "it passes at the next camp or inn". |
| Holding is free: no gold, supplies or time. Skipping is the worse option by design (-5 to +3 for the close). | Spec says loss should cost something besides gold. The cost is the grief and the loyalty at stake; the choice is whether to pay a moment of attention. No reward loop: a rite exists only for a real departure, once for each companion. |
| Loyalty changes: close companions +3 held or -5 skipped; the rest of the band +1 or -2. Close means a bond of at least "trusts" (25) with the departed, fixed at the loss. Held never lifts loyalty above 80 and skipped never lowers it below 30 (the opinions package's own ceiling and floor; a test pins both). | Same scale as opinions (a nudge of 5, clamp 30-80). The floor means a skipped rite can never be what makes someone desert; the ceiling means rites cannot buy unbreakable loyalty. |
| A held rite also draws each pair of close companions together (+2 bond, 1 hour cooldown, never above "close" through time-together limits). | Spec: "steadies those close (65)". |
| It is a chronicle line either way (`held` or `skipped`). | Phases 68 and 73 can read both; a company that let someone go unmourned is a deed too. |
| No effect on a fight, no experience, no gold, no power, no world time. | Rites must not become a difficulty lever (attached rule) or an economy loop. |
| Mourners are living company members who can bond (not creatures, not the departing). A mourner who deserts or dies before the rite is skipped silently. | Same people bonds already count. |
| At most five rites wait; a sixth drops the oldest silently. | A company would have to lose five companions between two camps; a hard bound keeps the record small. |

## Folded-in check: does a member knocked out in a won fight always take a lasting wound?

No, and it is left that way. Ashveil has no knocked-out-and-living state for a companion: at zero health a companion dies (`MobDeath`), and a death clears their wounds (`keptState`, now pinned by `TestAFallenCompanionKeepsNoWounds`); raising them costs a level and returns them at half health with no wounds, and an unraised one is lost (and now mourned). Lasting wounds come from a critical blow that gets through armor, a crushing blow's light wound, a defeat scenario (`left for dead`), a bad errand and a scene (`wounds.Beaten`). The leader falling is a defeat scenario, not a knockout. Pillars' injury rule (a downed member always carries a wound) would double-charge a fall that already costs a level and the rescue clock, and would be a difficulty lever. Recorded, no change.

## Review (2026-10-07, Opus review thread)

Accepted and fixed:

1. **An inn's rite could not be held.** Holding needed a camp or a *resting* inn stay, and an inn stay lasts 60 seconds. A leader who read the offer and `help rites` first found the stay over; paying for another stay then let the rite pass at its cost. Rites can now be held anywhere tagged `inn`, paid stay or not (`riteBusy`; `TestRitesThroughTheRealRoundTickAndCommands` checks the real check and the Rites tab's buttons in an inn room). Help updated.
2. **A rite that passed unanswered read as the leader's own choice** ("You let Hild go without a word"). It now says no rites were held and the moment passed (`TestACampOffersThenLetsAnUnansweredRitePassAtTheNext`).
3. **"At the fire" wording at an inn.** The hold line, offer text, card tooltip and help no longer assume a fire.
4. **The bonds restart bug could recur.** The fix is sound (the decoder now reads `bonds` and `rites`; every other saved field was already read). `TestWireRecordReadsEverySavedRecordField` now fails if a future `Record` field is saved but not decoded.
5. **The scrolling sub-tab bar hides its scrollbar**, so on a narrow desktop dock a mouse could not reach the hidden tabs. A vertical wheel over the bar now scrolls it sideways (browser check added).

Checked, no change: the creed seam stays clean (no god named; words live in `internal/rites`); no gold, supplies, experience or combat power; loyalty stays in 30-80; the rite and the loss share one save, and a failed save rolls both back (offline-leader path too: `registry.Lose` keeps the queued rite); desertions through bonds rivalry, morale and `restoreForLeader` all go through `removeCompanion`.

Rejected: a confirm prompt on Let pass (the cost is small, clamped, and the button is labeled); limiting camp rites to the camp's room (a leader with a camp pitched elsewhere is a rare, harmless case).

Follow-up: `scripts/browser/dock-windows-check.mjs` "bestiary: an open entry stays open across a refresh" failed once in three runs here (passes on master too; timing, unrelated to rites). `make smoke` failed once on "help keyring renders nothing" (a stock page) while the race suite ran alongside, then passed alone.

## Not done, on purpose

- No creed words or faith-specific rites (phase 73).
- No gold or supply cost to hold, no rest bonus or penalty, no rite for the leader's own death (defeat scenarios cover it).
- No rite for an errand-away or separated mourner beyond the loyalty change (they are mourners like any other).
- A rite that is never answered because the leader never camps or visits an inn simply waits.
