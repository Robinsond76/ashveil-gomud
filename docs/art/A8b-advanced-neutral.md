# A8b: Advanced classes of the neutral lineages

Read [00-standards.md](00-standards.md) first. For each class, attach its
**lineage's approved A1 map sheet and A6 battle idle**. Neutral lineages
are open to any alignment. Their routes keep the lineage accent and are
told apart by **gear and silhouette**, not by alignment trim.

Each class needs **two files**:

- `art/source/A8b/map/units/<id>.png`: a map figure sheet, 3 × 8.
- `art/source/A8b/battle/units/<id>/idle.png`: a battle idle sheet, 1 × 4,
  size M, facing right.

A class that fights beside a creature (a hound, bear, drake, doll or
golem) is drawn **alone**. Its creature has its own battle sprite (A7).
