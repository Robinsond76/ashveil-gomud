# A1: Base class map sprites

Read [00-standards.md](00-standards.md) first. Attach the approved A0
anchors, especially `lineup-map.png`.

Make one **map figure sheet** per row: **3 rows × 8 columns** (down, up,
side facing right; 2 idle and 6 walk columns), laid out as in standards
section 4. These figures walk across the map tiles. Every promoted class
that has no art of its own falls back to its lineage's figure, so these
15 sheets cover the whole game until A8.

Each figure must match its A0 lineup figure exactly: the same design in
motion. The neutral lineages and the adventurer weren't in A0, so match
its scale, grain and proportions.

## Notes

- The **side row faces right**. Weapons can switch hands when the game
  mirrors it; that's expected.
- Long weapons (halberd, spear, staff) may rise above the head. Keep the
  whole figure about 5 heads tall, with the weapon extra.
- The witch and warrior samples the owner shared already exist. Redeliver
  them in this layout, with the two idle columns added, as PNG with true
  transparency.
