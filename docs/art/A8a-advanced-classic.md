# A8a: Advanced classes of the classic lineages

Read [00-standards.md](00-standards.md) first. For each class, attach its
**lineage's approved A1 map sheet and A6 battle idle** as references. An
advanced class is that figure promoted: the same body and silhouette
family, with new gear and the route's alignment cue (standards section 1).

Each class needs **two files**:

- `art/source/A8a/map/units/<id>.png`: a map figure sheet, 3 rows × 8
  columns (standards section 4).
- `art/source/A8a/battle/units/<id>/idle.png`: a battle idle sheet, 1 × 4,
  size M, facing right.

"Gate" is the route's alignment: **+** positive (pale or tarnished-gold
trim), **any** unrestricted (keeps the lineage accent), **−** negative
(ash-black and ember red).

## Warrior lineage (from `warrior`)

| ID | Gate | Role in play | Design |
|---|---|---|---|
| `knight` | + | An armored protector who heals by laying on hands | Plate over mail, a kite shield with a pale sun device, a slate surcoat with gold-trimmed hem, a bone-white plume. Battle: shield high, sword ready. |
| `mercenary` | any | Physical disruption, a hard tackle | Mixed, mismatched armor and an ochre gambeson. A heavy blade carried on the shoulder, a coin pouch and a leather baldric. Battle: blade on the shoulder, weight forward. |
| `blackguard` | − | An armored protector who heals by spilling blood | Dark iron plate with short spikes, a charcoal surcoat with an oxblood hem, a jagged axe. Battle: axe low, shield scarred. |
