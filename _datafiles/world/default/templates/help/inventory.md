# Help for ~inventory~

The ~inventory~ command gives you a summary of your characters equipment.

It leads with your company's load: your personal and cargo weight against your carrying capacity, with the cargo part called out separately, and how much food and drink you're carrying between you. That's a separate thing from the "Carrying" line's item count, which just limits how many items you can hold.

You can search for a specific object by entering its full (or partial) name:  
~inventory wood~ would list items with "wood" in the name.  
~inventory wearable~ would list all wearable items in your inventory.

## Usage:

  ~inventory~  
  ~inventory [search term]~
