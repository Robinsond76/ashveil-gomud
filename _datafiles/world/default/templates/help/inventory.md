# Help for ~inventory~

The ~inventory~ command gives you a summary of your characters equipment.

It leads with your company's load: the weight your company carries against its capacity, with the cargo part called out separately, and how much food and drink you're carrying in your own pack (your companions' packs and cargo aren't counted there). The "Carrying" line counts your items; there's no limit on how many you hold, only on how much the company can carry (see ~help cargo~). Once the company is full, you can't pick anything more up.

To see what everyone in the company carries, type ~company inventory~.

You can search for a specific object by entering its full (or partial) name:  
~inventory wood~ would list items with "wood" in the name.  
~inventory wearable~ would list all wearable items in your inventory.

## Usage:

  ~inventory~  
  ~inventory [search term]~

All unworn items are shared cargo. Use ~help equipment~ for assignments and comparisons.
