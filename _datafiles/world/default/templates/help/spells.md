# Help for ~spells~

The ~spells~ command lists spells you have learned, along with some useful information about them.

## Usage:

  ~spells~

  To get help on a particular spell, try: ~help spell [spellId]~

Damage and healing spells grow with your level and Mysticism; ~help spell [spellId]~ shows how one grows, and each level-up names what grew. A spell you know never fizzles in a battle. Wizards learn Shower of Sparks, clerics Minor Heal All and witches Earthbind at level 3.

Minor Heal All targets your present company even without a player party. Single-target help uses the named patient; other players are never automatically included. Targets are rechecked when the chant finishes. See ~help friendly-effects~ for scope, eligibility and costs.

~cast sigil of [kind]~ is not a spell but a set-up: it lays a sigil before a fight (see ~help sigils~).

**See also:** ~help cast~, ~help sigils~
