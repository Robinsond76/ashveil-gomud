# Help for ~sell~

The ~sell~ command sells an item to merchants you are visiting.

## Usage:

  ~sell sword~  
  This would sell a sword, the merchant wants it and has the gold.

  ~sell junk~  
  Sells everything you have marked as junk in one go, and plain junk-type
  items such as scrap, to the merchants here. Anything no one wants stays in
  your pack. See ~help mark~.

## What a merchant pays

A merchant buys only the kinds of thing they already trade in, at a share
of its worth (never more than a quarter), and pays less for more of the same
as they pile up (the pile thins out as the days pass). ~offer~ asks the
price first.

Generated gear sells too: its worth rises with its quality and, once read,
its affixes. Gear whose properties are unknown (Rare and better, not yet
identified) sells at a discount, priced by its rarity alone, so you can
never tell from the price what it carries. Reading it first usually pays
(~help identify~). Gear with a blob of data, or a spent item with uses
used up, is not bought.

Settlement markets buy goods at prices that move with what they hold
(~help market~), and smiths turn unwanted gear into materials
(~help salvage~).

Find out more about referring to items by name by typing ~help item-names~.
