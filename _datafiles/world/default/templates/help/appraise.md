# Help for ~appraise~

The ~appraise~ command will pay a merchant a fee to get basic information on an item.

## Usage:

  ~appraise sword~  
  This would be equivalent to using the ~inspect~ skill. It costs 20 gold.

  ~appraise [unidentified item]~  
  A merchant will also read gear whose properties are unknown, and lay its
  affixes bare for good. The fee grows with its rarity: 60 gold for a Rare,
  150 for an Epic, 400 for a Legendary or Set. A Scribe in your company reads
  gear for free (~help identify~).
