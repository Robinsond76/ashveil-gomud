# Help for ~eat~

The ~eat~ command eats an item you carry (if it is edible).

If the food restores Hunger or Thirst, eating it feeds you, or a named
companion instead of you.

## Usage:

  ~eat sandwich~  
  This would eat your sandwich.

  ~eat sandwich tamsin~  
  This would eat your sandwich to feed the companion named Tamsin
  instead of yourself. Name a companion, ~#~ and their roster number
  (like ~#2~), or ~leader~/~me~/~self~ for yourself.

Some food only fills a stomach; some also carries water and restores
Thirst too. Whoever eats it, you'll be told what it did for them: how
full or how comfortable they now are. See ~help survival~ for what
Hunger and Thirst mean and how they're spent.

Find out more about referring to items by name by typing ~help item-names~.
