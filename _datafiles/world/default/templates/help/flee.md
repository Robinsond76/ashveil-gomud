# Help for ~flee~

The ~flee~ command attempts to run away from combat into another area. In a
battle it is the only way out: nothing else you type changes the battle. A
flight that gets away ends your battle at once.

If successful, foes will not chase you. You must escape every foe attacking you, however.
Your companions follow you out.

The chance of success is **30 + (fleeSpeed / (fleeSpeed+attackerSpeed) * 70)**

## Usage:

  ~flee~
